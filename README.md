Schizophrenia is one of the most misunderstood conditions in the world. Many people and families never find clear, kind, trustworthy information, especially in Bangladesh.

That was my idea: a simple app anyone can open on their phone and actually get help from. I planned it, chose the content and shaped the design. I built it with Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant.

Meet Mindscape 🌅 Here is everything inside, and how it helps:

🧠 Signs and symptoms: the 7 main signs in plain language, so families can notice changes early.
💊 Core treatments: medication, therapy (CBT), family support and specialty care, so people see what real treatment looks like.
🌿 Add-on approaches: vitamins, omega-3, melatonin, yoga, diet and herbal options. Each shows the study, the year and how strong the evidence is, and each is clearly marked as an add-on, never a replacement for treatment.
🪞 Myths vs facts: tap-to-flip cards that challenge stigma, like "split personality" and "dangerous".
🚨 "Do I need help now?": a quick check that shows whether something can wait, whether to call a doctor today, or whether to call emergency services.
📞 Bangladesh helplines: 999, 16263 and Kaan Pete Roi, each linked to a Google search so people can verify before calling.
🌬️ Calm space: guided breathing and a 5-4-3-2-1 grounding exercise for hard moments.
🛡️ My safety card: save who to call, early warning signs and what helps. It stays on the person's own device.
☀️ Daily check-in: mood and sleep for 7 days, something to show a doctor.
📝 Questions for my doctor: a ready checklist for appointments.
🤝 Supporting someone you love: simple do's and don'ts for families.
📚 Over 20 references behind the content.

It also has light and dark mode, soft sunrise colours, no sign-up, and no data collected. Everything personal stays on the device.

Why it matters: stigma keeps people from asking for help, and families often do not know what to do. If this helps even one person understand the signs sooner, or feel less alone, it was worth building.

Important: Mindscape is for education only. It is not medical advice and not a replacement for a doctor. I am now looking for psychiatrists and psychologists to review the content, so if that is you, I would love your feedback.
